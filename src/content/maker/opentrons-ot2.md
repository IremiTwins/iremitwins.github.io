I spend my working life around liquid-handling robots, so naturally I now have one at home. The Opentrons OT-2 is a pipetting robot: an X/Y/Z gantry, a Raspberry Pi 4 running the show and a Smoothieboard motion controller. That makes it very hackable.

## The plan

The goal is **creative, non-lab uses**. Ideas on the list:

1. **Pen plotter** with a webcam mount — the first build.
2. Pick-and-place for small parts.
3. Solder-paste dispensing for PCBs.
4. Computer-vision-guided sorting.
5. Terrarium automation.
6. Cocktail mixing, eventually.

Custom tool holders and mounts will come off the [3D printer](/nika/maker/3d-printing).
