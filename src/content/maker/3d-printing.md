The Bambu Lab P1P is the workhorse of my hobby setup, and PLA Basic is my go-to filament.

## How I work

- **CAD:** I started in Tinkercad and have moved to **Fusion 360** as my main tool. I'm also exploring AI-assisted CAD, where OpenSCAD's code-based models are the easiest for an AI to edit.
- **Slicing:** Bambu Studio. I export **STL** for single-colour parts and **OBJ** when I want multi-colour prints through the AMS.
- **Magnets:** I've standardised on **8 × 3 mm N52** magnets for almost everything. Pockets printed at **8.1–8.2 mm** give a snug press fit in PLA.

## Lessons learned the hard way

- A recurring bed-adhesion problem turned out to be the **Z offset**, not the plate or the filament.
- I've recovered from a full spaghetti-and-blob failure. A **cold pull** is now part of my routine for clearing the nozzle.
